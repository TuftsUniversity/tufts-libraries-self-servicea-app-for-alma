Concurrent Checkouts

Upload a spreadsheet of checkout transactions like in this template to analyze whether their were loan bottlenecks for high demand titles with multiple items, such as laptops You can generate a report with the same fields as /shared/Tufts University/Reports/Fulfillment/Course Reserves/Tisch Laptops - Concurrent Checkouts, but use other conditions if you like

It will find out for every day how many unique items were out on loan, and how many total items were available, to help you analyze whether there were days where all items were out on loan, or if there were bottlenecks in the loaning of items. For simplicity of data presentation, it dooesn't show resolution at the hour or minute level, so it's possible one item was checked out and returned several times that day. But this should still help you understand loan and item availabiltiy bottlenecks for the set of items you provide
